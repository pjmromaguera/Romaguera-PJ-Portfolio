In Practice

Weighing what matters most

The hardest part of risk analysis isn't the model — it's weighing what matters most.

I design decision frameworks that turn multiple, often conflicting criteria into a single, defensible ranking — from sustainability scoring across health, climate, and economic indicators, to mapping how different hazards interact and compound with each other.

  • Designed a multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA) framework using AHP and TOPSIS to rank sustainability performance across SDG-aligned indicators.
  • Built a geospatial framework analyzing interactions between multiple hazards, including cascading and compounding effects.
  • Facilitated vulnerability & exposure workshops to align risk metrics with business impacts and adaptation strategies.
  • Computed closeness coefficients and produced ranked outputs for comparative sustainability assessment of plant-based oils.
